Piet Mondrian

He started his career painting traditional, naturalistic landscapes. Over time, his style evolved through Impressionism and Cubism until he stripped art down to its most basic elements

He believed that reducing art to pure geometric forms expressed a universal, spiritual order behind life

1872   Born in Amersfoort

1911   Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917   Co founds De Stijl with Theo Van Doesburg

1930   Paints composition with red, blue and yellow

1944   Dies in New York

Less, until it holds

Despite the precise, geometric look of his famous grid paintings, Mondrian drew all his straight lines by hand.

The Nazi regime detested modernist art, and Hitler's regime targeted Mondrian's work, featuring it in the infamous Degenerate Art exhibition

Born Pieter Cornelis Mondriaan, he shortened his surname to Mondrian around 1906 and dropped an "a" when he moved to Paris in 1911 to distance himself from his Dutch past and blend into the avant-garde art scene
